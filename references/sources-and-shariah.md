# Sources, freshness and Shariah

## Source hierarchy

Start official; discover current linked reports rather than guessing undocumented endpoints.

- PSX portal: https://dps.psx.com.pk/ — summary, trading panel, screener, company announcements, notices and listing status.
- Indices: https://dps.psx.com.pk/indices
- Screener: https://dps.psx.com.pk/screener
- Company profile: `https://dps.psx.com.pk/company/{SYMBOL}`. Follow official financials, announcements and corporate actions. Profile ratios are not a substitute for filed accounts.
- History: https://dps.psx.com.pk/historical and portal daily downloads. Prefer published traded value; label close-times-volume as an estimate.
- Methodology/membership: https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/product-and-services/indices and current recomposition notices.
- Islamic market: https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/resources-and-tools/islamic-capital-market and official Shariah portal linked from PSX.
- SBP: https://www.sbp.org.pk/ for current policy, MPC calendar, FX/reserves and economics. PBS: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/ for inflation/releases. SECP: https://www.secp.gov.pk/ for regulatory decisions. Use company IR, ministries, NCCPL and CDC as relevant.
- Reputable dated Pakistani/international financial reporting can establish news context. Prefer primary disclosures for company-specific facts. Analyst targets/social posts are not filed facts or independent rumour confirmation.

Official fetching may fail or return cached data. Try official downloads/filings, then reputable secondary sources. Label substitutions and unresolved conflicts. Never bypass access controls or silently promote an aggregator to official status. Materially conflicting prices, unexplained corporate-action discontinuities or unverifiable listing status block an active entry call.

## Freshness/history

- State analysis time in PKT, latest completed session and each quote's observation time. Retrieval time is not observation time. Friday's close retrieved on Sunday is end-of-day data, not live.
- Value/swing setups need latest completed-session prices and all subsequent material announcements. Older quotes permit historical analysis, not a current BUY.
- Intraday triggers require completed bars and quotes no more than 5 minutes old, known feed delay and current bid/ask or equivalent execution evidence. Delayed/unavailable feeds mean NO LIVE CALL; offer next-session conditional levels.
- Prefer 250+ daily sessions, require at least 60 valid observations for normal tactical screening. Defer moderate-risk tactical calls on new listings with inadequate history. Compute 200-day measures only with sufficient data. Compare intraday volume to equivalent time-of-day history, never a partial day to a full day.
- Verify splits, bonus shares, rights and ex-dividend adjustments. Use consistent comparable series for returns/indicators and current tradable prices for orders. Distinguish total-return and price performance; investigate dividend adjustments before calling a breakdown.
- Financials need latest publicly available annual/interim filings, actual periods and standalone/consolidated basis. Mark missing/delayed accounts. Check official warnings, suspension, default/non-compliant listing status and material enforcement. Listing compliance is separate from Shariah compliance.

## Shariah verification

Use latest effective official PSX-KMI All Share screening/recomposition and corrections, or a newer official issuer-specific Shariah decision. KMI-30 membership cross-checks status but is not exhaustive. KSE-100 membership says nothing about compliance; absence from KMI-30 proves nothing.

Tags:

- **COMPLIANT**: ticker/company matched to current effective official compliant result. Link notice, account/screening period and effective date.
- **NON-COMPLIANT**: effective official screening/decision explicitly identifies non-compliance.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no authoritative match, incomplete/conflicting evidence or unresolved provisional result. Never infer status from company name, ownership, sector or an old table.

Distinguish announcement, financial screening and implementation dates. Check objection windows, corrected notices and superseding decisions. Future recomposition must not overwrite current status early; show both when different. Reproduce official compliance ratings only with their dated meaning/source. Never invent ratings.

Version trap: PSX/N-244 (26 February 2026) announced a debt threshold change from 37% to 33%, a rating mechanism and objection process for implementation from the November 2026 recomposition. Reaching November does not itself establish an effective constituent list. Verify actual implementation and later amendments; hardcode neither threshold as permanently current.

Historical methodology anchor: https://dps.psx.com.pk/download/attachment/271129-1.pdf . This is not a current constituent list.

If officially available, show applicable purification ratio, period and provisional/final status. Never reuse an old ratio as current or invent religious rulings from missing inputs. Stock screening is not a fatwa and does not certify margin, short-sale, derivative or settlement arrangements.
