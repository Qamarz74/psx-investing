# Screening and calls

## Moderate-risk defaults

These are adjustable operating defaults, not PSX rules, validated optimal parameters or guaranteed risk outcomes. Disclose material overrides; never relax gates to fill a shortlist. Small allocations do not make high-risk stocks suitable. Low volatility alone is not a rejection: require attractive upside with moderate overall risk.

| Gate | Default |
|---|---|
| Integrity | Exclude suspension, active default/risk warning, material unresolved enforcement, negative equity and unresolved going-concern concerns. Read material audit qualifications/adverse opinions; assess their substance. |
| Liquidity | Median daily traded value over 20 completed sessions at least PKR 20m; traded all last 20 sessions; proposed order at most 1% of median daily share volume. |
| Volatility | Tactical ATR(14)/price at most 5%; penny at most 4%. Repeated unexplained circuit locks, gap exposure or poor exit depth override a numeric pass. |
| Quick execution | Current spread at most 0.5%, adequate depth and verified exit mechanism. Do not enter locked circuits. Verify tick sizes, price limits and broker stop capabilities. |
| Tactical stop risk | Normally 0.5% of supplied portfolio equity per trade, 1.5% combined open tactical risk; penny at most 0.25% per trade. Account for correlated/overlapping positions. Gaps can exceed planned loss. |
| Concentration | Normally at most 10% capital per name, 25% per sector; pennies at most 2% each, 5% combined. No invented capital or allocations if holdings/equity unknown. |
| Trade quality | At least 2:1 reward/risk after estimated round-trip costs to a defensible primary target. Nearby resistance before 2R blocks BUY even if distant arithmetic T2 reaches 2R. |

Missing gate inputs mean NOT VERIFIED, blocking active tactical BUY. Distinguish user preferences from defaults. If the user explicitly changes the mandate, label departures rather than mislabelling them moderate risk.

Check earnings, board meetings, ex-dates, SBP decisions and material binary events. Default wait through a material event within the next 2 sessions for new swing/quick entries; explain any evidence-based exception and residual gap risk. Long-term holdings need event analysis, not a claim all events can be avoided.

## Long term: 12–36 months

- Review at least 3 financial years plus latest interim/TTM where available. Prefer consolidated group economics with attributable/diluted EPS unless another basis is justified. Adjust per-share comparisons for corporate actions.
- Assess normalized sales/EPS, margins, ROE/ROIC, cash conversion, debt service, liquidity, dividends and capital allocation. Separate tax reversals, disposals, revaluations, FX gains, subsidies and other one-offs. Normalize cyclical profits.
- Use sector-specific valuation/quality: bank asset quality, coverage, capital and sustainable ROE/PB; cyclical mid-cycle profits; holding-company NAV/discount; energy collections and receivables. Industrial cash-flow/debt metrics do not map directly to banks.
- Require sustainable profitability and resilient finances. Losses, poor collections and unreliable accounts can make a cheap multiple a value trap.
- Build bear/base/bull valuation ranges using justified normalized multiples, DCF or NAV and dated inputs. Cross-check another lens when feasible. Explain rate, PKR, commodity and margin sensitivity as relevant. No unsupported fixed PE or unverified growth formula.
- Prefer an entry about 25% below defensible base fair value, a plausible value-realisation path and acceptable downside. This is a margin-of-safety default, not a price floor. Unreliable inputs mean WATCH rather than invented fair value.
- Give entry band, catalysts, financial thesis invalidation, review date and valuation-based trim/sell rules. Do not keep broken theses because the horizon is long or price fell.

## Short term: 3–20 sessions

- Use daily structure/weekly context, 20/50/200-day averages when available, Wilder RSI(14), Wilder ATR(14), volume and 20/60-session relative strength. Indicators are corroboration, not independent proof of an edge.
- Relative strength = stock return minus benchmark return over identical dates/basis. Use KMI-30 for compliant core names and KSE-100 broadly; disclose benchmark/sector mismatches.
- Prefer base breakout/retest, orderly uptrend pullback or a verified catalyst with structure/volume confirmation. Reject chasing spikes, insufficient upside to resistance and excessively distant stops.
- Specify exact pending/completed confirmation, such as daily close above sourced resistance then a holding retest. Pending triggers are CONDITIONAL BUY. Normally require confirmation volume at least 1.5x 20-session median; otherwise remain conditional.
- Place stop beyond defensible structural invalidation with stated volatility buffer. Derive targets from actual supply/resistance, not fabricated levels that happen to reach 2R. Recompute reward/risk at available entry, not an earlier unavailable price.
- State partial-profit proportions and residual stop/trailing rule when suggesting trims. Never widen a stop or relabel a failed tactical trade long term.
- Reassess no follow-through after 3 sessions; close/re-plan at stated maximum horizon. Pending entries expire after 2 sessions unless otherwise stated.

## Quick profit: intraday to 2 sessions

- Intraday requires fresh timestamped bars, spread and depth. Otherwise use EOD-based conditional 1–2-session plans; never infer VWAP/opening range from daily closes.
- Define session, completed-bar trigger, structural stop, resistance targets and flatten deadline before that session's close. Default intraday flat, no overnight. Explicit 1–2-session plans need overnight event/gap assessment and maximum exit date.
- Use breakouts/retests/VWAP reclaim only when actual bars establish them. Verify both Friday sessions and special schedules. Compare same-time-of-day volume where possible.
- Require 2R net to achievable primary resistance, acceptable execution and event risk. A price touch is not a fill; stops can slip/fail at price limits. Disclose manual exits if broker lacks stop support.

## Penny-stock lane

Default research definition: latest price at or below PKR 50, configurable. This is not an official PSX classification or evidence of cheapness.

- Require positive comparable 20/60-session returns, outperformance of KSE-100 across both windows and a constructive current base/pullback. One-day gain or high turnover alone does not qualify.
- Apply all common gates plus median 20-session daily traded value at least PKR 50m, positive latest TTM earnings and positive equity. Check cash generation, dilution/rights, promoter concentration and free float using sector-appropriate analysis.
- Reject rumour-driven runs, unexplained serial circuit locks, official manipulation warnings, extreme volatility, trading gaps and poor exit depth. Describe observed warning signs; never accuse an issuer of manipulation without evidence.
- Tactical only unless independently passing full value/quality analysis. If none qualify: "No penny stocks qualify under the moderate-risk filters."

## Calls and calculations

- **BUY**: fresh evidence shows trigger satisfied, entry still within band and relevant gates passed. Value BUY requires supported entry valuation and intact thesis. Label EOD calls; next opening fill remains unknown.
- **CONDITIONAL BUY**: precise pending trigger, entry band and expiry; not entry now.
- **HOLD**: existing holding's plan/thesis remains valid; not a fresh-entry call. Without holdings use WATCH.
- **TAKE PROFIT**: target/valuation reached or justified trim; give proportion and residual exit. Do not assume ownership.
- **SELL**: existing holding's stop/thesis failed or exit rule met. Without holdings use AVOID / EXIT IF HELD; never imply a short sale.
- **AVOID / WATCH / NO TRADE**: failed gates, insufficient upside/data. Explain what would change the call.

For long tactical entry E, stop S, primary target T: gross R = (T-E)/(E-S). With estimated per-share entry cost Ce, stop-exit cost Cs and target-exit cost Ct: net R = (T-E-Ce-Ct)/(E-S+Ce+Cs). Include spread/slippage and fees in estimates; require positive numerator/denominator. Without cost inputs, disclose gross R and keep conditional until conservative costs are stated. Tax depends on current rules/user circumstances; never invent a universal capital-gains rate.

With stated capital/risk budget, quantity is the smallest permitted by stop risk including costs, cash/concentration and liquidity, rounded down to verified lot size. No position size from price alone. No numeric win probability or expected profit without suitable out-of-sample validation including costs, delistings and historical membership. A screen is not that validation.
